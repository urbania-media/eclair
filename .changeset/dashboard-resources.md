Manage projects, API keys, storages and global presets in the dashboard, and see usage and costs. A project's page has its settings, its webhook secret (shown on demand, and changed with a grace period), its keys (a new one is shown once; revoke one) and its storages (add or replace one, without showing credentials again; remove one). The usage page shows each project's task time per day and over a period, its share of a monthly server cost, and exports the lines as CSV.
