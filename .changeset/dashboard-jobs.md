Add the jobs and the overview to the dashboard. The jobs of every project, filtered by status, project, operation, creation date or metadata, with cancel and retry. A job's page shows its request, its probed input, its tasks on a timeline, its webhook deliveries and its JSON. The overview, now the dashboard's home, shows the server's readiness, queues and hosts, and over a period its tasks, operations, latest failures and webhooks. Storages get an access test, and the projects list their jobs, failures and webhooks over 24 hours.
