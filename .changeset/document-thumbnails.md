Add `@eclair-js/operations-document` with `document.thumbnails`: one JPEG or PNG image per page of a PDF, or for some pages, rendered by poppler. The defaults are those of laravel-mediatheque's document `Thumbnails` job (every page, 150 dpi, JPEG at quality 100). The server loads the package.
