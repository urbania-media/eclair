Add `@eclair-js/operations-font` with `font.convert`: WOFF2 and WOFF from a TrueType, OpenType, WOFF or WOFF2 font, with fonttools, optionally subset to code points or a text. It replaces the WOFF and WOFF2 files of laravel-mediatheque's `WebFonts` job. The server loads the package.
