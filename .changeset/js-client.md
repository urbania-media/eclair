`@eclair-js/client`: the JavaScript client of the `/v1` API (jobs and their events, probes, direct and resumable uploads, presets, operations), with `EclairError`, and the verification of webhooks and `http` storage requests. `@eclair-js/server` exports `startTestServer()` from `@eclair-js/server/testing`, to test a client against a real server.
